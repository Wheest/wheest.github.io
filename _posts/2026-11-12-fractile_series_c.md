Yesterday, [Fractile](https://fractile.com)
[announced](https://x.com/fractile_ai/status/2120519162925654070) that we've
raised $720M in a Series C, led by Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed and
Redpoint, with further participation from new and existing investors. The FT
[covered it too](https://x.com/FT/status/2120516640076947624).

<!--more-->

From the announcement:

> Our first product-scale chip, which we will be manufacturing next year, will
> stack DRAM directly on top of the processor, combining radically better
> bandwidth and speed with the capacity needed to run the world’s largest models
> at long context.

Stacking DRAM on top of the processor is a key differentiator, among others. I
hope to be more candid in future, but for now, co-designing the hardware and
software stack is a big part of both our technical and cultural ethos.

It's been interesting to be a part of it up until this point. But we're still so
early. [Join us](https://fractile.com/jobs)!
